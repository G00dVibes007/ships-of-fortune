Ships of Fortune Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Your saved game

Ships of Fortune saves your game only on your own device, in the app's private storage. That save is deleted when you uninstall the app. The game has no accounts and no sign-in.

Leaderboard

The game has an optional Rankings tab that lists the top captains by gold earned. If you choose to join it, the game sends two things to our leaderboard server: the captain name you pick, and your gold score. A random ID created on your device lets the server update your own entry and no one else's.

The captain name and score are shown publicly on the leaderboard. We don't collect your real name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information, and we don't sell or share leaderboard data with anyone. The server is hosted by Supabase, and all data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

If you'd like your leaderboard entry removed, email us at the address below with your captain name and we'll delete it.

Ads

Ships of Fortune shows optional ads through Google AdMob (for example, "Watch an ad: half price" at the shipyard). To show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. This information is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), you'll be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).

Analytics

Ships of Fortune itself does not use any analytics.

Children

Ships of Fortune is intended for players aged 13 and over.
