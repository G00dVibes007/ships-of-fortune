Ships of Fortune Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Your captain's code and saved game

Ships of Fortune has no sign-in. Instead, the game gives each player a random captain's code. Your profile, which holds the username you choose, your best gold score and a copy of your saved game, is stored on our game server under that code, so you can load it on another phone by entering the code. Anyone who has your code can load your profile, so keep it private. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of the code.

Your game is also saved on your device, in the app's private storage, and that copy is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Leaderboard

The game has a Rankings tab that lists the top captains by gold earned. If you attach a username, your username and gold score are shown publicly on the leaderboard. We don't collect your real name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information, and we don't sell or share your profile or leaderboard data with anyone. The server is hosted by Supabase, and all data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Deleting your profile

In the game, open Rankings and tap Delete my profile. That wipes your username, score and online saved game from the server straight away, removes you from the leaderboard and retires your code. Or email us at the address below with your captain's code or username and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob: you can choose to watch one to save your ship after a lost battle, to open a high-value contract (once every 3 hours), or for half-price ship upgrades. You never have to watch an ad to play.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, diagnostics and app performance. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first and can change it any time from "Ad privacy settings" in the Shipyard. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads).

The game itself has no analytics.

Links to other apps and sites

Links at the bottom of the game open our VibeChat app's page on Google Play and the LCBC church website (lcbcchurch.com). Tapping them sends nothing from the game; those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Ships of Fortune is intended for players aged 13 and over.
